Does a committed record keep the human who approved it?

Every row is an approval the graph really performed; "retained" is decided by searching the ledger fact the store actually kept for the approver the graph recorded. A record with no human approval never appears here at all, so a dropped approver cannot be mistaken for nobody having approved.

3 of 3 human approvals do not survive into the ledger: the commit fact is built from the advisor's proposal :value, while the approver was written to the approval payload alongside it. The audit log therefore records THAT a harvest record was logged, but not WHO authorised it.
